2. No Server Collection / 100% On-Device Processing

The Takt application operates entirely offline and locally on your device. Actimi GmbH does not maintain servers that collect, store, or process your personal health records. We do not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide any credentials to external services.

3. Local Alarms & Notifications

Medication reminders are scheduled directly by your smartphone's operating system (iOS / Android) as local notifications and system alarms. No server sends push notifications, and Actimi GmbH has no knowledge of your scheduled times or when alarms fire.

4. Reports and Data Export

When you choose to generate a doctor report (PDF) or export your history, the file is compiled entirely on your device and handed over to your operating system's native share sheet. Actimi GmbH receives zero copies and has no access to exported files. You retain sole control over who you share the exported files with.

5. Family Sharing (Feature Preview)

The family sharing interface is currently provided as a feature preview. To protect user privacy while end-to-end synchronization architectures are completed, invitations are disabled in this release and no data is transmitted across servers.

6. No Tracking, Analytics, or Advertising

Takt contains no advertising, no tracking SDKs, no third-party analytics services, and no advertising identifiers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data in any form.

7. Retention & Complete Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively in your device's local memory, your records remain on your smartphone only for as long as you keep the app installed. You can edit or delete entries at any time in the app, or remove all data permanently and without residue by uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage.

8. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Under Chapter III of the GDPR (Articles 15–21), you have rights regarding access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing. In Takt, you exercise these rights directly on your own device with full autonomy.
