Privacy Policy

Takt Health app · Version 1 · September 2026

In short. Takt stores your medication plans and the doses you confirm, skip or miss, so it can remind you and show you your record. Health data is processed only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app. Data is hosted in the European Union on the Ovok health platform. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No adverts, no tracking, no analytics, no sale of data.

3. Data that stays on your iPhone

These settings and working data never leave your device: language, appearance, snooze length, reminder sound, spoken reminder, “ring as alarm”, follow-up reminder, whether names are hidden on the lock screen, supply counts and refill dates, scheduled reminder identifiers, and a small diagnostic log of reminder events that contains no health information. A copy of today’s schedule is kept on the device so reminders work offline. Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain. All of it is removed when you delete the app.

4. Reminders, alarms and spoken reminders

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone as local notifications and, if you turn on “Ring as alarm” (iOS 26 or later), as system alarms through Apple’s AlarmKit. No server of ours sends them or knows when a dose is due. A reminder shows the medication name and time on your lock screen unless you choose “Hide name”. Spoken reminders use the speech engine built into iOS.

5. Reports and exports

The doctor report (PDF), the medication list (PDF) and the history export (CSV) are created on your iPhone only when you ask for them and handed to the iOS share sheet. We do not keep a copy. Whoever you share them with is responsible for them from then on.

6. Family sharing

Family sharing is optional. When you invite a relative, you give a separate explicit consent for that person. Your relative needs their own Takt account and sees, read-only, today’s doses and whether you confirmed them. They cannot see your history, your reports or your notes and cannot change anything. The access is a permission on your record, not a copy. You can revoke it at any time in Settings → Family sharing; it ends immediately, and the grant and revocation dates are kept as an audit trail.

8. No tracking, no adverts, no profiling

Takt contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs and no advertising identifiers, and we do not sell data. There is no profiling and no automated decision-making. Figures in your history, such as how many doses were taken, are calculated for you to see and are not used for anything else.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21). Many of these you can exercise directly in the app: edit or archive a medication, correct a dose entry, and export your data as CSV or PDF.

Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Consent → Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)). It takes effect immediately and stops further processing of your health data; processing before the withdrawal remains lawful. To have your account and stored data deleted, write to [[CONTACT EMAIL]]. We answer requests within one month.

12. Security

14. Not a medical device

Takt is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or dosing advice.
