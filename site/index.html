Takt Health

Calm medication reminders and a clear record of what you took.

Download on the App Store

Free · iPhone · English and German · No adverts, no tracking

What it does

  • Reminders that get throughAlarm sound or spoken reminder, a quiet follow-up, snooze, and Taken or Snooze straight from the notification. On iOS 26 and later, reminders can ring as an alarm, even in silent mode or Focus.
  • Today at a glanceA timeline of what is due and what is done. Confirm all at once, take a dose early, skip with a reason, undo a tap.
  • Flexible schedulesDaily, chosen weekdays, chosen days, every few days or as needed. Intake instructions, course end date, tablet shape and colour, pause until a date.
  • Supply trackingKeep count of what is left and get a note when supply runs low.
  • HistoryA 14-day chart, a weekly summary, corrections for past entries and CSV export.
  • For your appointmentA doctor report as PDF for 7, 14 or 30 days, and a one-page medication list.
  • Family sharingInvite a relative to see today’s doses and whether they were confirmed. Read-only, with your explicit consent, revocable at any time.
  • Private by designHide medication names on the lock screen. Data stored in the EU. No adverts, no analytics, no data selling.

Built for everyone: text never smaller than 17 pt, full VoiceOver support, light and dark mode.

Takt is a reminder and a record. It is not a medical device and does not give medical or dosing advice.
