Figures in your history describe what you recorded; they are not a clinical assessment.

4. Reminders can fail

Reminders are delivered by your device’s operating system. They may be late or not appear at all, for example if notifications or alarms are not allowed for Takt, in silent mode or Focus (unless “Ring as alarm” is on, iOS 26 or later), when the device is off, out of battery or has the wrong time, after an operating-system update, or when the app was removed. Do not rely on Takt as your only reminder for medication where a missed dose could be dangerous.
