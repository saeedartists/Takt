Frequently asked questions

My reminders don’t ring. What should I check?
  1. iPhone Settings → Notifications → Takt Health: allow notifications, sounds and lock-screen alerts. Turn on Time Sensitive Notifications if shown.
  2. In Takt: Settings → Reminders. Make sure “Reminder sound” is on.
  3. Check that your iPhone shows the correct time and time zone.
  4. Open Takt once after restarting your phone or updating iOS, so reminders are rescheduled.
  5. Low Power Mode and Focus can delay or silence notifications. See the next question.
How do I hear reminders in silent mode or Focus?

On iOS 26 or later, turn on Settings → Reminders → “Ring as alarm”. Takt then schedules each dose reminder as a system alarm through Apple’s AlarmKit, which rings like an alarm clock even in silent mode or Focus, whether Takt is open or closed. If you declined the permission, allow it in iPhone Settings → Takt Health → Alarms. On earlier iOS versions reminders arrive as notifications; add Takt Health to the allowed apps of your Focus instead.

What are the follow-up reminder, snooze and spoken reminder?

Follow-up reminder sends one quiet second reminder if a dose is still open. Snooze reminds you again after the length you set. Spoken reminder reads the reminder aloud while the app is open. You can press Taken or Snooze directly on the notification. All are under Settings → Reminders.

Can I hide medication names on the lock screen?

Yes. Settings → Reminders → Reminder text → “Hide name”. Reminders then say “Time for your medication”.

How do I add a medication?

Open the Medications tab and tap “Add medication”. Enter the name, the time or times and how often: daily, on chosen weekdays, on chosen days, every few days or as needed. Optionally add strength, intake instructions, a course end date, tablet shape and colour, and your supply. You can later pause a medication until a date, duplicate it or archive it.

I took a dose early, skipped one, or tapped the wrong button.

On Today, use “Take early” for an upcoming dose, “Skip” with a reason, or “Undo” right after a tap. Older entries can be corrected in History.

How do I export a report for my doctor?

Open the Doctor report (from History or Settings), choose 7, 14 or 30 days and export it as PDF. The same screen exports a one-page medication list, and History offers a CSV export. Files are created on your iPhone and shared through the share sheet; we do not keep a copy.

How does family sharing work?

Settings → Family sharing → enter your relative’s name, relationship and e-mail → confirm the consent statement → “Invite by email”. Your relative accepts in their own Takt account and then sees, read-only, today’s doses and whether you confirmed them. Nothing else. To stop, open Family sharing and tap “Revoke access”; it ends immediately.

How do I withdraw consent or delete my data?

Settings → Consent → “Withdraw consent” stops further processing of your health data immediately. To delete your account and all stored data, write to [[CONTACT EMAIL]] from the e-mail address of your account. Deleting the app removes the settings stored on your iPhone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which languages does Takt support?

English and German. Takt follows your iPhone language; you can change it in Settings → Appearance → Language.

Does Takt tell me what dose to take?

No. Takt is not a medical device and gives no medical or dosing advice. It reminds you of the plan you enter and records what you report. Please ask your doctor or pharmacist about your medication.

Takt is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition and gives no dosing advice. Always follow your doctor’s or pharmacist’s instructions.
