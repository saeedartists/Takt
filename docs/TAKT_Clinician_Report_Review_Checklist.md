## Review checklist

### A) Clinical clarity
- [ ] Patient identity is clear enough for practical use.
- [ ] Date/window context is clear.
- [ ] Overall adherence percentage is easy to interpret.
- [ ] Per-medication rows are understandable and actionable.
- [ ] Missed-dose section communicates risk-relevant behavior without overstatement.

### B) Safety and interpretation boundaries
- [ ] No wording implies diagnostic or treatment recommendation.
- [ ] Report is clearly adherence/logging evidence, not clinical decision support.
- [ ] Disclaimers are sufficient and understandable.

### C) Usability in real workflow
- [ ] One-page format is practical for consultations.
- [ ] Font sizes and spacing are readable in print/PDF.
- [ ] Terminology is suitable for clinician-patient discussion.
- [ ] Information density is appropriate (not too sparse / not overloaded).

### D) Localization quality (EN/DE)
- [ ] English wording is natural and unambiguous.
- [ ] German wording is natural and unambiguous.
- [ ] No clinically meaningful translation drift between EN and DE.
