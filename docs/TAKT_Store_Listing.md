## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Derived from the code: account and health data go to the Ovok FHIR API (`src/lib/ovok-fetch.ts`); preferences, supply counts and reminder bookkeeping stay on the device (AsyncStorage/SecureStore); no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK in `package.json`; no push token is requested (reminders are local notifications and AlarmKit alarms).

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.

| Data type | Collected | Linked to the user | Used for tracking | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health & Fitness → **Health** (medication plans, dose log, skip reasons) | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Contact Info → **Name** (account holder; name of an invited relative) | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Contact Info → **Email Address** (sign-in; relative's invite address) | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Identifiers → **User ID** (account / FHIR patient id) | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Everything else (location, contacts, photos, browsing, search, purchases, financial, usage data, diagnostics, advertising data, device ID) | No | – | – | – |

- Tracking: **No.** No data is used to track users across apps or websites, no IDFA, no ATT prompt needed.
- Check before submitting: the app contacts Expo's update service (`u.expo.dev`) on launch with IP address, platform and app version. We do not use that data, so it is not declared above. If you use EAS Update insights/metrics, declare Diagnostics → Other Diagnostic Data, not linked, App Functionality.
- Google Play Data safety: same declarations, data encrypted in transit, deletion on request.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")

```
Takt Health is a medication reminder and dose log. Users enter their own medications; the app reminds them at the chosen times and records what they report (taken, skipped, missed). It gives no medical advice, no interaction warnings and no dosing recommendations, and it is not a medical device.

Demo account
E-mail: [[DEMO ACCOUNT EMAIL]]
Password: [[DEMO ACCOUNT PASSWORD]]
The account already has sample medications, so the Today, History and Doctor report screens show data. On first launch after sign-in, the app shows the health-data consent screen (explicit consent under GDPR Art. 9); tap "I agree and continue".

Reminders
Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device with the user's notification permission. No push server is used. The notification offers Taken and Snooze actions.

AlarmKit (iOS 26+)
Settings > Reminders > "Ring as alarm" is an opt-in setting. When on, Takt schedules each dose reminder as an AlarmKit alarm so it rings through silent mode and Focus, the way a medication alarm should. The system asks for alarm permission when the user turns the setting on (NSAlarmKitUsageDescription). To test: turn it on, add a medication with a time two minutes from now, lock the phone with the ring/silent switch on silent.

Family sharing
Settings > Family sharing lets the user invite a relative (second account: [[SECOND DEMO ACCOUNT EMAIL / PASSWORD]]) who then sees today's doses and their status, read-only. It requires a separate explicit consent and can be revoked at any time.

Data
Account and health data are stored in the EU on the Ovok health platform. Preferences stay on the device. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Contact: [[REVIEW CONTACT NAME, PHONE, E-MAIL]]
```
